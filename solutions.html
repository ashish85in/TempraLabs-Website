Heat risk intelligence

From heat signal to safer work decisions.

Weather-sensitive operations are not only shaped by storms and flooding. Extreme heat can affect crews, outdoor work, campuses, events, construction, utilities, logistics, and public services.

Tempra can help translate site-specific heat conditions into safer work windows, exposure awareness, supervisor alerts, and records of protective actions.

Site-specific heat

Translate changing heat conditions into location-aware risk for exposed people and operations.

Safer work windows

Identify when work can continue, when controls may be needed, and when escalation is warranted.

Protective action records

Support supervisors with alerts, documented actions, and clearer accountability during heat events.
