The opportunity

Weather data is abundant. Operational clarity is not.

Most forecasts describe the hazard. They do not explain which crews, sites, assets, or communities should move to the top of the list.

Tempra adds operational context, turning environmental signals into a focused view of exposure, consequence, and action.

The operational gap

Forecasts identify the hazard. Operations still have to decide what to do.

When conditions change, leaders need more than another layer on a map. They need a shared view of what is exposed, how serious the consequence may be, and where intervention matters first.

  1. Which people, places, and assets are in the path?
  2. What is vulnerable, and what is the operational consequence?
  3. Which action should happen first?

The Tempra approach

A decision layer for weather-sensitive operations.

Connect forecasts to operational context, then surface the priorities a team can act on. The platform is designed to support multiple hazards and sectors without becoming another general-purpose weather dashboard.

First application

HeatSafe

Plan safer work before heat disrupts the day.

HeatSafe turns forecast heat risk into practical planning guidance for outdoor and heat-exposed work. Teams can see when risk is likely to rise, who may be affected, and where schedules or controls may need attention.
