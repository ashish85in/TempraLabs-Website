Mission, Vision & Approach

Mission

Turn advanced weather and climate intelligence into practical decisions that help organizations protect people, assets, services, and infrastructure.

Vision

A future where weather-sensitive organizations can anticipate risk, prioritize action, and operate with confidence as extremes become more frequent and consequential.

Approach

Start with the decision, connect environmental signals with operational context, and translate risk into clear priorities for the teams responsible.

Research Foundation

Informed by deep science and funded R&D experience.

Tempra Labs is shaped by Ashish Sharma's prior work leading and contributing to climate, weather, infrastructure, and decision-support initiatives supported by organizations such as NSF, DOE, NASA, the Walder Foundation, and utility-sector partners. That experience informs the company's technical judgment while keeping Tempra Labs' commercial work independent from those research sponsors.

Federal R&D

Prior research programs supported by NSF, DOE, and NASA built experience in high-resolution modeling, impact forecasting, and infrastructure risk.

Public & civic

Foundation-supported and civic-facing work helped translate climate intelligence into decisions that affect communities and critical services.

Utility context

Utility-sector engagement informs how storm risk, asset exposure, crew planning, and operational response need to connect before disruption.
